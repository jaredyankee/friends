Privacy Policy

Draft — not yet in effect. Friends has not been released and collects no data from anyone today. This page documents what the app is being built to store, so the final policy can be written against something accurate. It is not a legal agreement, has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and will be replaced before launch.

What the app is designed to store

DataWhy
Email address and authentication credentials To create and sign in to your account.
Profile: handle, display name, avatar, time zone So friends can find you, and so times display correctly in your zone.
Schedule items: times, recurrence, and optionally title, location, and notes The calendar itself. Detail fields are optional and are only shown to people you have explicitly granted that level of access.
Visibility settings and sharing grants To enforce who can see what. Enforced on the server, not in the app.
Friend connections and group membership To build group calendars and shared availability.
Group messages Group chat.

Availability is calculated, not stored

Friends never stores a record of when you are free. Free time is derived on request by subtracting your schedule items from a time range, and what another person sees depends on what you have shared with them. Two people can see different availability for you, and that is by design.

Sections still to be written

Before launch this policy needs: how long data is retained, third-party processors and subprocessors, how to request deletion or export, children's use, international transfers, contact details for privacy requests, and the effective date.

Deleting your account

Account deletion will be available inside the app. What happens to messages you have sent to a group, and to a group whose last owner deletes their account, is still being decided — the answer will be stated here explicitly rather than left to inference.
