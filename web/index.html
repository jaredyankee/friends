Find the time that works for everyone.

Friends is an iOS app for coordinating plans. Keep your schedule, share it with the people you choose, and see the windows when your whole group is actually free.

  • Share as much or as little as you want — a blocked-out hour, or the full details.
  • Group calendars combine everyone's schedule and highlight when you're all free.
  • Tap any window to see exactly who's available.
